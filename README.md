# Rajapandian Kanagaraj

**Quantitative Ecologist | Ecological Modelling | Biodiversity & Global Change**

I use quantitative and spatial modelling to understand how environmental variation and biotic interactions shape biodiversity and ecological dynamics across spatial and temporal scales.

## Research

My research spans several ecological systems and focuses on understanding the processes underlying species distributions, community assembly, and individual and population dynamics.

- **Biodiversity, Community Assembly & Spatial Ecology**
- **Environmental Change, Species Distributions & Population Dynamics**
- **Plant Demography, Biotic Interactions & Ecological Modelling**

My current research as a postdoctoral researcher at the Helmholtz Centre for Environmental Research (UFZ) focuses on neighbourhood effects on plant performance in tropical forest dynamics plots.
